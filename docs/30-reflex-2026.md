---

## 1. Purpose

REFLEX 2026 will be the first real Event Edition used to move TSDW Sports from a frontend prototype into an operational sports platform.

The platform should allow students to follow REFLEX through a public website while authorized TSDW Sports staff manage fixtures, scores, results, competition progression, and event standings.

The primary goal is to have a usable production version available before REFLEX begins.

REFLEX should use the same generalized TSDW Sports architecture intended for future events such as TSpark and National Sports Day.

REFLEX-specific requirements must not be implemented by breaking or bypassing the documented platform domain model.

## 8. Competition Formats

The system must not assume every Competition uses the same tournament format.

Potential formats include:

```text
KNOCKOUT
LEAGUE
GROUP_KNOCKOUT
MULTI_PARTICIPANT
LEADERBOARD
```

Additional formats may be introduced later.

### Knockout

A participant or team is eliminated after losing according to the Competition rules.

Example:

```text
Quarter Final
    ↓
Semi Final
    ↓
Final
```

### League

Participants play multiple matches and accumulate standings.

Example:

```text
             P   W   D   L   PTS

COMP         5   4   1   0   13
BVOC         5   3   1   1   10
IT           5   2   2   1    8
```

### Group + Knockout

Participants compete in groups before qualifying for a knockout stage.

### Multi-Participant / Leaderboard

Multiple entrants may participate in the same match, lobby, round, or session.

Results may depend on placement, eliminations, points, or another Competition-specific scoring system.

This is particularly relevant for games that do not naturally fit a two-team fixture model.

## 9. Match Series

Competitions may use formats such as:


The exact series format for each REFLEX Competition is currently TBD.

The platform must not assume BO1 globally.

## 17. Fixture Status

The system should support states including:

```text
SCHEDULED
LIVE
COMPLETED
POSTPONED
CANCELLED
WALKOVER
```

Additional states may be introduced if required by the generalized domain model.

A scheduled Fixture must never display a fabricated score.

---

## 18. Postponement

Authorized Event Staff may postpone a Fixture.

A postponement should preserve relevant information such as:

```text
Fixture
Status: POSTPONED
Original scheduled time
New scheduled time: datetime | TBD
Reason
Updated by
Updated at
```

The original Fixture should not be deleted simply because it was postponed.

Public presentation may expose only information appropriate for participants and spectators.

---

## 19. Disqualification

Authorized Event Staff may disqualify an Entrant where required by event rules or conduct decisions.

Disqualification must not be represented as an ordinary numeric match result.

A disqualification should support information such as:

```text
Entrant
Status: DISQUALIFIED
Reason
Competition
Actioned by
Actioned at
```

The system must preserve enough information to understand the resulting Competition progression.

Public and internal disqualification reasons may differ if required.

---

## 20. Walkovers

The system must support walkovers.

A walkover must:

- identify the advancing/winning Entrant,
- avoid fabricated numeric scores,
- clearly communicate the result.

Example:

```text
COMP     W/O
BVOC      —
```

A walkover is a result state, not a numeric score.

---

## 21. Winners and Runner-Ups

REFLEX Competitions should support final placements including:

```text

Runner-Up
```

Additional placements may be supported later where required.

Competition results must remain distinct from Event-level department standings.

## 36. Architecture Principle

REFLEX 2026 is the first production Event Edition, not a standalone REFLEX website.

Features introduced for REFLEX should strengthen the generalized TSDW Sports platform.

The target architecture remains:

```text
TSDW Sports
│
├── Events
│    │
│    ├── REFLEX
│    │     ├── 2026
│    │     └── Future Editions
│    │
│    ├── TSpark
│    │     ├── 2027
│    │     └── Historical Editions
│    │
│    └── National Sports Day
│
├── Official Teams
│
├── Achievements
│
└── Organization / Committee
```

The system should support REFLEX without introducing architecture that prevents future TSpark or other TSDW Sports requirements.
