# TSDW Sports

A sports event management, live scoring, records, and operations platform developed for TSDW Sports at Thakur College of Engineering and Technology.

## Status

🚧 Early Development

The project is currently in the product planning and system design phase.

## Documentation

Project documentation is available in [`/docs`](./docs).

## Planned Capabilities

- Sports event management
- Fixtures and tournament management
- Live scoring
- Results and winners
- Department standings
- TCET sports teams and achievements
- Committee records
- Participant management
- E-certificates and verification
- Historical sports archive
- Role-based administration
